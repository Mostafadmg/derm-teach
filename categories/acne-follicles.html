    Acne & follicles

    Disorders of the pilosebaceous unit, with NICE NG198 treatment ladders, antibiotic stewardship and the acne mimics.

    Acne vulgaris

    Comedones, papules, pustules and nodules of the pilosebaceous unit. Follow NICE NG198 combinations for 12 weeks, never use antibiotic monotherapy, and refer early for scarring.

    Never miss: Acne fulminans (systemically unwell, ulcerating): same-day dermatology. Scarring and distress: refer early.

    Acne mimics: rosacea, perioral & folliculitis

    Rosacea (no comedones), steroid-induced eruptions, perioral dermatitis, and Gram-negative, Malassezia and Pseudomonas folliculitis.

    Never miss: Rosacea fulminans (explosive central face in a young woman) and ocular keratitis — urgent ophthalmology / dermatology. Do not keep cycling antibiotics for Gram-negative folliculitis.

    Acanthosis nigricans

    Velvety brown thickening of the neck and axillae — usually a marker of insulin resistance; rarely a paraneoplastic red flag.

    Never miss: Sudden extensive AN in a slim older adult, especially with tripe palms or oral involvement → urgent suspected-cancer pathway.
