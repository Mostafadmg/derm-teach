    Cosmetic & hair

    Acne scars and keloids, pattern hair loss, alopecia areata and scarring alopecias, plus counselling-level aesthetic topics (neuromodulators, solar lentigines, IPL).

    Acne scars & keloids

    Atrophic (ice-pick, boxcar, rolling) vs raised (hypertrophic, keloid) scars, scar vs pigment, and which specialist modalities suit which scar. Prevention is treating active acne early.

    Never miss: Scarring at any acne severity is an NG198 referral trigger. Also look for psychological distress and keloid tendency before procedures.

    Androgenetic alopecia (male & female)

    Male pattern: recession plus vertex. Female pattern: a widened parting with the frontal line kept. Topical minoxidil for both sexes, finasteride 1 mg for men, and an honest view of PRP and off-label options.

    Never miss: Scarring alopecia (lost follicle openings, inflammation), hyperandrogenism in women, and telogen effluvium triggers (iron, thyroid).

    Cosmetic procedures: peels, needling & lasers

    A counselling guide to peel depths, microneedling and laser classes, and an evidence-based view on procedures during or after isotretinoin (the old blanket 6-month wait is outdated).

    Never miss: PIH and keloid risk in skin of colour, HSV reactivation, and unregulated providers. Do not ‘clear’ isotretinoin patients yourself.

    Facial ageing & neuromodulators

    Counselling-level guide to intrinsic vs extrinsic ageing, dynamic vs static lines, and botulinum toxin / filler safety — no injection protocols.

    Never miss: Filler vascular occlusion (pain, livedo, visual loss) → emergency pathway. Suspected iatrogenic botulism after unlicensed toxin → 999 / UKHSA.

    Solar lentigines

    Benign sun-induced lentigines versus freckles, melasma and seborrhoeic keratoses — with lentigo maligna as the red-flag differential.

    Never miss: Irregular, multicoloured, enlarging facial pigment → lentigo maligna — urgent suspected-cancer referral. Never laser an undiagnosed pigmented lesion.

    IPL & eyelash enhancement

    Counselling on intense pulsed light for pigment and vessels (including hypopigmentation risk) and bimatoprost for eyelash growth.

    Never miss: Never IPL an undiagnosed pigmented lesion. Eye injury risk without proper protection. Bimatoprost can darken iris permanently.

    Alopecia areata

    Autoimmune non-scarring bald patches with exclamation-mark hairs. Many limited patches regrow; refer extensive, childhood or distressed disease.

    Never miss: Scaly childhood patch = tinea until proven otherwise. Loss of follicular ostia = scarring alopecia — urgent dermatology.

    Trichotillomania

    Habitual hair pulling causing irregular patches of broken hairs. Habit-reversal therapy is first-line; watch for trichophagia and trichobezoar.

    Never miss: Trichophagia with abdominal pain, vomiting or mass → urgent surgical assessment for trichobezoar.

    Scarring alopecia overview

    When follicular openings are lost, hair loss is permanent. Recognise LPP, FFA, DLE, FD and CCCA and refer early — primary care holds the line while waiting.

    Never miss: Scarring alopecia is irreversible once established. Also refer non-healing nodules on bald scalp via suspected-cancer pathways.

    Telogen effluvium

    Diffuse shedding of club hairs 2–3 months after a trigger. Reassure, find reversible causes, and cross-link pattern hair loss rather than duplicating AGA photo sets.

    Never miss: Patchy loss, scale, or lost ostia are not TE — rethink AA, tinea or scarring alopecia.
