    Inflammatory skin

    Chronic, relapsing inflammatory dermatoses seen every week in primary care: eczema/dermatitis and psoriasis.

    Eczema / dermatitis

    Itchy, barrier-driven inflammation. Treat with generous emollients and a topical steroid matched to site and severity, and never miss eczema herpeticum.

    Never miss: Eczema herpeticum: punched-out erosions in an unwell patient. Admit the same day.

    Psoriasis

    Sharply defined, scaly plaques caused by IL-23/IL-17 inflammation. Manage with topical steroid and vitamin D, steroid breaks, and screening for joints and comorbidities.

    Never miss: Erythroderma or generalised pustular psoriasis (same day), and joint symptoms suggesting psoriatic arthritis.

    Urticaria & angioedema

    Wheals that itch and fade within 24 hours, with or without angioedema. Regular non-sedating antihistamines (up-dosed if needed); never miss anaphylaxis or ACE-inhibitor angioedema.

    Never miss: Anaphylaxis (ABC) → adrenaline/999. ACE-inhibitor angioedema without wheals — stop the ACEI urgently. Lesions lasting >24 h with bruising → urticarial vasculitis.

    Contact dermatitis

    Irritant vs allergic contact eczema — avoidance, emollients, stepped topical steroids, and when to refer for patch testing. Deepens the contact themes on the eczema page.

    Never miss: Occupational hand dermatitis needing workplace adjustments; airborne allergy presenting as facial/eyelid eczema; do not use aqueous cream as leave-on.
