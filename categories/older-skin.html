    Ageing skin & older adults

    Itch and xerosis, skin fragility and dermatoporosis, actinic keratoses and photoageing in older adults.

    Itch & xerosis in older adults

    Asteatotic eczema, venous and discoid eczema, lichen simplex and prurigo — with scabies and bullous pemphigoid as don’t-miss itch differentials.

    Never miss: Itch out of proportion to rash — think scabies, bullous pemphigoid, and systemic disease (CKD, cholestasis, lymphoma).

    Skin fragility & dermatoporosis

    Actinic purpura, thin skin, skin tears and stellate pseudoscars — protect fragile skin and review anticoagulants/steroids carefully.

    Never miss: Palpable purpura, non-exposed-site bruising, mucosal bleeding or safeguarding patterns — investigate / escalate.

    Actinic & seborrhoeic keratoses

    Rough sun-damaged AKs and field-directed treatment options versus harmless stuck-on seborrhoeic keratoses — and when to refer.

    Never miss: Tender, enlarging, ulcerated or hypertrophic lesions — rule out SCC. Pigmented facial “lentigo” that is irregular — think lentigo maligna.

    Photoageing

    UV-driven dermatoheliosis: solar elastosis, Favre–Racouchot, poikiloderma of Civatte, IGH and related patterns — prevention first.

    Never miss: Photoaged fields hide AK, BCC, SCC and lentigo maligna — examine the whole field, not just the wrinkle.
