    About this review

    Revision key for the DermUSW end-of-module exam for Module 2 (Dec 2023): 30 single-best-answer questions. Saved is the answer ticked before submission (not a graded course key). Recommended is the teaching answer on current UK guidance (NICE / CKS, BNF, BAD, BASHH, Resuscitation Council UK, UKHSA).

    1Young woman, rapidly growing bleeding papule after a scratch; granulation-like capillaries with epithelial collaretteLobular capillary haemangiomaLobular capillary haemangioma (pyogenic granuloma)Rapid growth after trauma, friable, collarette on histology. Amelanotic melanoma is the important DDx, so always send for histology. Bacillary angiomatosis needs HIV/Bartonella. Glomus is painful and subungual. KS is spindle cells and HHV-8—
    2Warty papules on the backs of hands/feet since childhood, "church-spire" hyperkeratosis, palmar pitsAcrokeratosis verruciformisAcrokeratosis verruciformis of HopfLinked to Darier disease (ATP2A2), which explains the palmar pits. EDV is HPV-driven flat warts with malignant potential. Stucco keratoses appear in older people. AK is sun-damaged older skin. Viral warts have no palmar pits and are not lifelong—
    358-year-old farmer, nodular flesh-coloured neck lesion with telangiectatic rolled edge; next step to establish the diagnosisShave biopsyExcision biopsy (likely course key)Looks like nodular BCC. UK practice: diagnostic excision with a 4 mm margin for a well-defined primary BCC (BAD BCC guideline, NICE CSG8) gives diagnosis and cure together. If unsure, a punch/incisional biopsy is used. Shave biopsy misses depth/subtype for nodular lesions. Tru-cut is for deep masses, triamcinolone is for keloid, and microneedling is cosmetic—
    432-year-old, 6 weeks pregnant, nodular BCCPostpone until after the birthPostpone excision until after deliveryBCC is slow-growing and rarely metastasises, so elective surgery can wait. Local anaesthetic is safe if surgery is needed, but there is no urgency. Cryotherapy is unsuitable for nodular BCC. Radiotherapy in pregnancy is inappropriate—
    560-year-old man, "moles of concern" on his back (image)Junctional naevusAtypical (dysplastic) naevus is more likelyNew or flat junctional naevi are unusual at 60, because naevi mature to compound or intradermal with age. Irregular, multiple lesions in an older adult fit atypical naevi. Use the image: if one lesion meets the ABCDE/7-point checklist, think melanoma and refer on the suspected-cancer pathway (NICE NG12)—
    6Bluish-black plaque since childhood, new satellite, blue-white veil, atypia, S100/Melan-A positiveMalignant blue naevusMalignant blue naevusA melanoma arising in or resembling a blue naevus: change, a satellite and atypia. LMM is facial and chronic sun damage. Pigmented BCC is S100-negative. KS is a vascular tumour—
    7Nodular melanoma: Breslow 4 mm, ulcerated, mitoses 1/mm²; strongest predictor of survivalBreslowBreslow thicknessAJCC 8 T-stage is based on Breslow + ulceration, and Breslow is the strongest single predictor. Clark level is no longer used for staging. Mitotic rate was dropped from AJCC 8 T1 staging—
    8Pacemaker (recent battery, regular review), BCC on abdomen >5 cm away; electrosurgeryPre/post checks + monopolarBipolar short bursts + monitoring + arrest trolley + inform cardiac physiology. Option 4 is the likely course key; option 1 (bipolar + pre/post checks) is acceptable and more cautiousBipolar current stays between the forceps tips, so pacemaker interference is minimal. Monopolar current runs through the patient and can cause oversensing or inhibition. With a stable, recently checked pacemaker (not an ICD), routine pre/post checks aren't needed if there are no clinical concerns. ICDs need deactivation. Options 2, 3 and 5 all use monopolar—
    9Renal transplant, multiple yellowish facial papulesSebaceous hyperplasiaSebaceous hyperplasiaCiclosporin is a classic driver. Umbilicated yellow papules with "crown" vessels on dermoscopy. Transplant patients also get many SCCs and BCCs, so examine everything. Fibrofolliculomas suggest Birt-Hogg-Dubé. Syringomas are periorbital. Trichoepitheliomas are skin-coloured and central-facial—
    10Gardner syndrome, punctum, malodorous expressible contentsEpidermoid cystEpidermoid cystMultiple epidermoid cysts in FAP/Gardner (APC), so check colonoscopy surveillance. A punctum + keratin contents is diagnostic. Dermoid cysts are congenital lines of fusion. Milia are tiny. Hidrocystomas are translucent and periocular—
    1112-year-old boy, hard calcified angulated cheek papulePilomatricomaPilomatricomaChildren, head and neck, rock-hard, "tent sign", calcification. Pilomatrical carcinoma is rare and occurs in adults. Dermatofibroma is on the legs with a dimple sign—
    1268-year-old, multiple painless red papules increasing over years (image), on SGLT2i etc.Amelanotic melanomaCampbell de Morgan spots (cherry angiomas)Multiple, small, bright red, increasing with age, benign. Reassure. Amelanotic melanoma is usually a single, changing, often nodular lesion. HHT gives mucosal/digital telangiectasias with epistaxis. Pyogenic granuloma is solitary and bleeding. No drug causes this—
    1362-year-old street cleaner, firm indurated papillomatous lesion with central crust on the back of the hand for 2 monthsExcisionSurgical excisionLikely cutaneous SCC (or KA, which is treated as SCC): excise with a 4–6 mm margin (BAD cSCC 2020). Curettage alone, PDT and imiquimod are not for invasive SCC. Radiotherapy is for people unfit for surgery—

Melanoma in situ, so refer (NG12). Solar lentigo is uniform and stable. SK is stuck-on/waxy. Nodular melanoma is raisedSolar lentigines
    15Dorsal distal-phalanx cyst with nail groove, clear jellyDigital myxoid cystDigital (mucous) myxoid cystLinked to DIP osteoarthritis. Pressure on the matrix causes a longitudinal groove. Glomus is painful, blue and subungual. Subungual exostosis is hard and under the nailNail signs: psoriatic nails, Beau’s lines & clubbing
    16Lifetime malignant transformation of a single benign intradermal naevus<1 in 2000Smaller than 1 in 2000The per-naevus risk is tiny (estimates ~1 in 200,000/year). Total naevus count matters more than any single mole—
    1770-year-old, 1 year of itchy worsening rash unresponsive to emollients and TCS (image), on ramipril/amlodipineSkin biopsySkin biopsyPersistent "eczema" that fails treatment in older age: exclude mycosis fungoides/CTCL (and drug eruption), which needs multiple biopsies.     1838-year-old woman, longstanding mole, now itchy; dermoscopy imageBlue naevusBlue naevus (as the dermoscopy image showed homogeneous steel-blue pigment)A homogeneous blue structureless pattern is typical. Itch and a family member with melanoma warrant documented review, not alarm—
    19Painful solitary calf nodule for 4 years; eosinophilic tumour, Masson trichrome positiveEccrine spiradenomaLeiomyomaMasson trichrome stains smooth muscle red. Pilar leiomyomas are painful (cold, touch). Painful skin tumours mnemonic "ANGEL" (Angiolipoma, Neuroma, Glomus, Eccrine spiradenoma, Leiomyoma). Of these, only leiomyoma fits the trichrome. Multiple leiomyomas raise the possibility of HLRCC (fumarate hydratase), so consider genetics—
    2020-year-old, previously pigmented mole now changed (white halo)Halo naevusHalo naevusA symmetrical depigmented rim around a regressing naevus, common in young people (vitiligo link). Refer if the central naevus is atypical or the patient is older—
    2122-year-old, rapidly growing exophytic bleeding hand lesion over 3 weeksCurettage and cauteryCurettage and cautery (send for histology)Pyogenic granuloma. C&C removes it and gives tissue for histology. Steroids, antibiotics and reassurance are inappropriate—
    2260-year-old runner, firm slightly raised forehead lump of 6 weeks (image)BCCBCC vs keratoacanthoma/SCC, depending on the imageSix weeks of growth and firmness favour KA (manage as well-differentiated SCC). A pearly rolled edge with telangiectasia would favour BCC. Either way the UK route is excision and histology, not watchful waiting. AK is flat/scaly. LM is pigmented—
    2370 kg; maximum 1% lidocaine with 1:200,000 adrenaline49 ml49 ml7 mg/kg with adrenaline × 70 = 490 mg = 49 ml of 10 mg/ml. Plain lidocaine max is 3 mg/kg (≈21 ml). Use the BNF/local policy—

INR 3.6 is above the usual skin-surgery threshold (≤3.5, checked within 72 h). Missing doses or stopping for 3 days without the anticoagulation team risks VTE—
    2554-year-old sunbather, enlarging, well-demarcated, red, scaly, crusted plaque on each lower legActinic keratosisBowen's disease (SCC in situ)A well-demarcated red scaly plaque on the lower legs of a woman is classic Bowen's. AK is a rough, ill-defined small macule or papule on the head and hands. UK management: 5-FU, imiquimod, PDT, cryotherapy, or C&C (BAD 2014 guideline)Actinic & seborrhoeic keratoses

    Where the course answer differs from UK practice

    • M2 Q3 (BCC diagnosis): shave biopsy is not ideal for nodular BCC. Use diagnostic excision (4 mm margin) or punch biopsy (BAD BCC guidance).
    • M2 Q8 (pacemaker): UK dermatological surgery teaching says bipolar. Routine pre/post checks aren't needed for a stable pacemaker; ICDs differ.
