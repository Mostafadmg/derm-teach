    Where the course answer differs from UK practice

    • M4 Q5 (PV): the course says "urgent referral and start prednisolone". UK specialists use rituximab + prednisolone first line (BAD 2017). In primary care, start steroids only after a specialist discussion.
    • M4 Q2 (child's facial eczema): none of the options matches NICE CG57. Potent TCS on a child's face is not UK practice.
    • M4 Q14 (generalised AE flare): the UK step-up is to a more potent TCS for the body plus TCI or mild TCS for the face. Oral steroid courses are reserved for severe flares, and TCIs are second line (NICE TA82).
    • M4 Q8 (pollen and AE): "mast cells" is a type I framing. Eczematous inflammation is Th2 T-cell driven.
