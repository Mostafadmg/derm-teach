    About this review

    Revision key for the DermUSW end-of-module exam for Module 5 (Jun 2024): 30 single-best-answer questions. Saved is the answer ticked before submission (not a graded course key). Recommended is the teaching answer on current UK guidance (NICE / CKS, BNF, BAD, BASHH, Resuscitation Council UK, UKHSA).
