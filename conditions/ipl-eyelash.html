    Cosmetic & hair

    IPL & eyelash enhancement

    Module 6 · Week 5

    Counselling on intense pulsed light for pigment and vessels (including hypopigmentation risk) and bimatoprost for eyelash growth.

    CoversIPLintense pulsed lightphotofacialbimatoprostLatisseeyelash growthhypopigmentation
    Think of it when

    Someone booking a “photofacial” for sun spots and redness, or asking about prescription eyelash growers.

    What drives it

    IPL uses broad-spectrum light absorbed by melanin and haemoglobin (selective photothermolysis). Bimatoprost prolongs the eyelash anagen phase via prostamide pathways.

    Backbone of care

    IPL only after lesion diagnosis and skin-type assessment; patch test; strict aftercare SPF. Bimatoprost: counsel iris/periocular pigment and glaucoma-drop origins.

    Never miss

    Never IPL an undiagnosed pigmented lesion. Eye injury risk without proper protection. Bimatoprost can darken iris permanently.

    What it is

    Intense pulsed light (IPL) is a non-laser flashlamp delivering broad-spectrum light through filters to treat vascular redness, solar lentigines and unwanted dark hair, with modest texture benefit.

    Eyelash enhancement options include cosmetics, lifts and bimatoprost (a prostamide also used in glaucoma drops) applied to the upper lid margin to lengthen lashes — counselling points below.

    2

    How it’s caused

    • IPL: selective photothermolysis of melanin and oxyhaemoglobin; dermal heating may remodel collagen modestly.
    • Darker or tanned skin absorbs more epidermal energy → higher burn and pigment-change risk.
    • Bimatoprost: increases eyelash length, thickness and darkness by extending anagen; periocular skin and iris melanocytes can also respond.
    3

    Clinical features

    • IPL sessions often 3–6, weeks apart; treated pigment darkens like “coffee grounds” then flakes over 1–2 weeks.
    • Adverse: burns, blistering, hypo-/hyperpigmentation (sometimes grid footprints), scarring, paradoxical hair growth, eye injury.
    • Bimatoprost: longer darker lashes over weeks to months; may cause periocular hyperpigmentation, iris colour change (especially hazel/green), itch, redness, fat atrophy (glaucoma-dose context).
    4

    Diagnosis

    Before IPL: confirm the diagnosis of every pigmented spot; Fitzpatrick type; recent tan; photosensitising drugs; HSV history. Melasma is generally a poor IPL candidate.

    5

    Management (UK pharmacist / IP)

    IPL counselling (UK)

    • Check practitioner training, insurance, patch test and (in Wales) HIW registration for Class 3B/4 lasers/IPL in independent clinics.
    • Contraindications/cautions: undiagnosed pigmented lesions, active infection/HSV, recent tan, photosensitivity disorders, many photosensitising drugs; extra caution in skin types IV–VI and on isotretinoin (many delay treatment).
    • Aftercare: SPF, gentle skincare, no picking; seek review for blistering or infection.
    • Cross-link solar lentigines and photoageing.

    Bimatoprost eyelash counselling

    • Explain cosmetic vs glaucoma origins; apply only as directed to the upper lid margin — not into the eye for cosmetic use.
    • Warn about permanent iris darkening, reversible periocular skin darkening, possible eyelash growth on unintended skin if product migrates, and ocular irritation.
    • Avoid if active ocular inflammation unless an ophthalmologist agrees; remove contact lenses as per product advice.
    • Effects reverse over weeks to months if stopped.
    • This page does not cover compounding, dosing schedules or off-label periocular aesthetic protocols beyond licensed counselling themes — check current SmPC/BNF.
    7

    Red flags & referral

    • Eye pain or visual change during/after IPL → urgent ophthalmology.
    • Blistering burns or striped hypopigmentation — stop further treatment; specialist wound/pigment advice.
    • Undiagnosed changing pigmented lesion — cancel IPL; refer.
    8

    Prescriber pearls

    • IPL is versatile but unforgiving on tanned or darker skin without expert settings.
    • Coffee-ground darkening is expected — picking prolongs marks.
    • Eyelash serums sold cosmetically vary widely; prescription bimatoprost needs proper counselling on iris pigment.
