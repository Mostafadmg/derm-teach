      Prescriber pearls

      Cross-cutting principles

      • Formulation is the intervention. Choose the base (ointment, cream, gel, foam) and scalp vs body products to suit the patient, because adherence follows acceptability.
      • Steroid ladder. Match potency to site and severity. The face, flexures and genitals need lower potency and shorter courses.
      • Infection is not the same as inflammation. Antibiotics are not automatic for crusted eczema or red legs.
      • Antimicrobial stewardship. No antibiotic monotherapy in acne, short courses for skin infections, and set review dates.
      • Skin of colour. Judge severity by scale, thickening, pigment change and symptoms, not erythema alone.
      • Psychosocial impact. Screen for distress in acne, psoriasis and eczema, and refer when needed.
      • Always check the current BNF, SmPC, MHRA alerts and local formulary or microguide before prescribing.
